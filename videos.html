My bigger videos go on my main channel while clips and smaller videos go on my second channel. I also stream on both Twitch and YouTube. YouTube is higher quality but has chat disabled, so go there if you just want to watch and don't want to interact.

I got interested in making YouTube videos after discovering the platform around 2008-2009. The idea that anyone could make anything they wanted and publish it for the world to see was fascinating to me, and what got me truly started was discovering Garry's Mod machinima videos like Idiot Box or kitty0706's works. Eventually I was inspired to make my own Garry's Mod video, Bananaphone Garry's Mod:

Fun fact, my original channel was called thepooman101 for several years. Do with that information what you will. Eventually I changed it to Retro Journey Videos because I was working on Inkagafar under Retro Journey by that point. In 2016 the channel changed name again to Retro Journey Creators, because I wanted to bring a few friends on board to make even more videos. This happened while I was at school, and there was another group I was friends with that were considering doing the exact same thing, so I proposed that we merged into one channel. They DID NOT take this well. The only way they were willing to accept the agreement was if we made an entirely new channel just for this project, so we did - that channel was Uninstalled, and it was an unmitigated disaster.

The core concept of Uninstalled was that every video or stream would feature at least two of us. I started the channel with a couple solo streams just to make sure I had everything set up and everything was working, and very quickly everyone else began to follow suit; that is to say, nobody did streams with anyone else. Instead of being a group channel, it was a channel with seven chucklefucks (actually just four) that would occasionally stream totally different things with completely different stream layouts.

There was no cohesion.

There was no unity.

Not even the thumbnail creation or video titling was standardised, so everything posted had a different design entirely. I wrote up a document explaining how the channel art was created so anyone could follow along and make their own assets that followed the design language I'd laid out from day 1, and began planning my departure with other documents that laid out the titling format I'd developed (among other things) to introduce any amount of consistency. I was also unhappy with the mentality the others had regarding the channel, as well as the participation of half the members (that is to say, none).

In 2017 I finally left alongside Dabee, one of my friends who I worked with regularly on Retro Journey Creators. From 2017 to mid-2018 I uploaded videos on my current channel, however it was under a different name at the time. The experience of working with Uninstalled had put me off working with other people so much that I was committed to working independently, a mindset I finally broke from around 2023.

Following my egg breaking in mid-2018 (iykyk) I requested all content I appeared in on Uninstalled to be privated. If you have seen any of my videos or streams, it will have been in this post-2018 period since very little (if anything) exists from before then.
