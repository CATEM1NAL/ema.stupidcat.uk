Software

Depending on what you're looking for, there's a lot I can list here!

Games:
> In 2010 I used RPG Maker VX to make a small game called Inkagafar: The Great Quest.

> From 2011 to 2014 I used RPG Maker VX Ace to work on a sequel (later changed to a prequel). While nothing came out of this, it did teach me the importance of planning ahead to avoid scope creep.

> In 2016 I used this knowledge to start writing a design doc for a new game set in the world of Inkagafar. A couple years later I picked it back up and began developing the game in RPG Maker MV - from 2018 to 2024 the game would go in and out of development whenever I had time and energy to work on it. On December 13th 2024 it would finally see a full release as Aretisia: Tenebris Dominus.

Game Mods:
> Since 2012 I've made 23 custom maps for Portal 2 and 7 custom maps for CS:GO alongside a ton of other stuff on the Steam Workshop.

> From 2014 to 2024 I made a ton of mods for the PAYDAY franchise on modworkshop.net, the two biggest being Various Text Changes (a complete UI rewrite for PAYDAY 2) and No Color Grading (disables PAYDAY 3's visual filter).

> In 2024 I wrote a Python script that implements haptic feedback into Jabroni Brawl.

> In 2025 I made 2 custom maps for Jabroni Brawl on GameBanana.

> In 2026 I contributed code to and provided documentation for an Archipelago implementation for Total War Warhammer 3. I then made my own implementation, a conversion mod for PAYDAY 2 called Criminal Dawn which was expanded out into a standalone full game overhaul, Thievenfall.

Tools:
> In 2016 I wrote a basic text encryption program called ENDCRYPT in Python, and over a few iterations I decreased the key generation from 1000+ lines of code to just 7.

> In 2023 I wrote a few Python scripts to calculate shots to kill in PAYDAY 3 (obsolete).
